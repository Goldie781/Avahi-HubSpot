How It Works

From Raw Data to
an Approved Draft

Five steps run in sequence. Nothing leaves your team until a scientist signs off.

  1. Source files

    Ingest

    Raw instrument output and lab notebook entries go in as your team captured them.

  2. Analysis

    Analyze

    Results are calculated and checked against the regulatory guidance the system is built on.

  3. Minutes

    Draft

    The agent writes a complete, reviewer-ready technical report in your format.

  4. Before review

    Flag Gaps

    Missing data is called out, the kind of gap that leads to a Refuse-to-File.

  5. Sign-off

    Approve

    A scientist reviews, edits, and approves. Every action is traceable.

Manual Drafting vs.
Agent-Drafted Reports

Same reviewers and the same regulatory bar. The difference is who writes the first draft.

Manual drafting

  • Scientists pulled out of the lab for weeks per report
  • Results copied by hand from instruments and notebooks
  • Gaps surface late, sometimes at the agency
  • Review cycles spent on formatting and consistency
  • Edits and approvals scattered across email threads

With Avahi's report agent

  • A complete draft from raw data in minutes
  • Data read directly from the source files
  • Gaps flagged against regulatory guidance before review
  • Reviewers spend their time on the science
  • Full traceability on every action
