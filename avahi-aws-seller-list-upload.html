    How It Works

    From Raw List
    to Ranked Pipeline

    You send the list. We score it, research the accounts worth your time, and return the report to you and your Avahi seller.

    Upload

    Submit a CSV with one row per company and a domain column. Add what you want us to target: a competing cloud, an industry, a company size, or a workload.

    Input: up to 300 companies

    Score

    Every account is scored 0 to 100 on competitive cloud displacement, modernization pressure, buyer access, firmographic fit, and timing signals, then placed in Tier 1 to 4.

    Output: score and tier per account

    Research

    Tier 1 and Tier 2 accounts are researched one by one for funding, hiring, stack, and leadership changes, so each card states why that account is primed.

    Output: 3 to 4 sourced signals per account

    Deliver

    You and your Avahi seller get the PDF report and a seller CSV with scores, signals, source links, and the technical buyer to reach at each priority account.

    Output: PDF report and seller CSV

    The Score

    Five Signals,
    One Score

    The weights favor what predicts a migration conversation: an account already paying for a competing cloud, with a reachable technical buyer.

    1. Competitive cloud displacement

      38 pts

      Azure, Google Cloud, or on-prem workloads in production. The strongest predictor of a live platform decision.

    2. Modernization pressure

      20 pts

      Legacy Microsoft web stacks, commodity hosting, and on-prem servers that raise cost and force a decision.

    3. Buyer access

      15 pts

      A named CTO, CIO, or VP of a technical function makes the account actionable this week.

    4. Firmographic fit

      15 pts

      Company size and current technology spend in the range where a migration pays back fastest.

    5. Timing signals

      15 pts

      Funding, cloud and DevOps hiring, AI initiatives, and new technology leaders in the last 12 months.
